Before choosing another tool, look at the journey.

Start with a specific situation

When a document is hard to find or an approval difficult to coordinate, a new platform can seem attractive. First, follow a real task: where it begins, whose hands it passes through and what each person needs to continue.

Distinguish the symptom from the cause

A duplicate file might come from the tool, an unclear versioning standard or a responsibility no one has agreed. Each cause needs a different response. Mapping the process makes it possible to ask more precise questions.

Connect the detail to its effect

The useful question goes beyond how much time a task takes. Understand what it displaces: a delivery, a decision, the ability to accept another commission or time with the client. That effect makes prioritisation possible.

Then choose what to change

Once the journey is visible, improvement may be simple: agree a standard, clarify an approval or connect two existing tools. If another platform is needed, there will be a specific reason to choose it and a way to assess its value.
