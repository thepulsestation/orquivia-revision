Custom applications · 3 MIN

Answer without rebuilding the project story.

If answering a client means cross-checking messages and spreadsheets, a view of milestones and outstanding items can bring together the context for a clear response.

In this article

01

One question interrupts several tasks

Imagine a project manager who needs to update the client about the next milestone. The calendar shows a date, a spreadsheet lists work in progress, and an email contains a decision that has not reached either place.

To give a reliable response, she checks with the team and compares the information. When the question repeats across projects, coordination consumes attention that deliverables also need.

02

Another dashboard does not resolve the cause

Slow responses are the symptom. The cause may be that each tool describes part of the work without linking tasks, decisions and deliverables. Adding a dashboard that needs separate updating can create another copy.

First, identify the information a response requires and where it comes from. A useful status distinguishes completed work, items awaiting a decision and the next commitment; it also shows when it was updated.

03

A view designed for answering

An application can bring together projects, milestones, owners and outstanding items by connecting available sources. Each milestone shows what is missing and the information behind it. Relevant changes trigger specific notifications without requiring a review of the entire portfolio.

If a source cannot be connected, manual entry is kept to what is essential, with a clear responsibility for updates. The internal view may include decisions that should not be shared with the client automatically.

04

Check the quality of the response

For a pilot, record how long status questions take to answer, how many internal checks are needed, and how often answers require correction. Compare similar questions after implementation. The improvement should make responses reliable and reveal blockers earlier, as well as reduce searching. Also check whether maintaining the view adds work: updates should fit the project’s existing workflow.

05

Start with a real question

Find the latest client question that required checking several sources. Note what you consulted and what was missing. Using that example, Orquivia analyses the workflow and proposes an initial view for a limited group of projects before considering an application for the whole practice.
