Milestones and documentation · 3 MIN

The work is delivered. What is missing to prepare invoicing?

In a hypothetical commission, the team completes a delivery, but the person preparing invoicing still needs to locate the sending confirmation, project identifier and acceptance status. The work is complete, yet its information remains scattered. That gap can extend the administrative preparation of a milestone.

Delivered does not mean prepared

Each commission may require different information or evidence to prepare this step. Distinguish completed delivery, recorded acceptance where applicable and complete administrative information. Combining them into one status hides what is missing and who should resolve it. A list based on the arrangements for the commission identifies whether delivery evidence or a project detail is missing.

The cause may precede delivery

If a commission identifier is entered in different ways, or nobody records where the evidence is stored, the final step inherits that inconsistency. Requesting more emails at the end addresses the symptom. A common reference and information recorded when it is created make preparation possible without reconstructing the whole project later.

A connection with a specific scope

An automation can gather the project reference, milestone, associated documents and available confirmations into a preparation view. If an item defined as necessary is missing, it flags it and assigns follow-up. When the information is complete, it triggers the review step. Review and authorisation to issue the invoice belong to the responsible person.

The view needs to show who reviews the information and when each status changed. For example, ‘documentation complete’ can remain separate from ‘review completed’. That distinction identifies the next step and avoids repeatedly notifying people who have already completed their part. Administration has a complete record for preparing the next step without requesting information the team has already recorded.

Check the time until information is ready

A useful measure is the interval between recorded delivery and the point when the required information is available for review. You can also observe returns caused by incomplete data and the queries needed. Compare equivalent milestones. The pilot should show whether the information for preparing invoicing is available earlier and requires less searching across tools.

Try it with a recent milestone

Choose a delivery and list the information the responsible person needed to prepare the next step. Mark which data already existed and which had to be requested again. Orquivia analyses those sources and can implement a connection or an outstanding-items panel. The first trial covers that administrative workflow, with criteria and responsibilities defined before development.
