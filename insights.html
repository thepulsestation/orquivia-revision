8 ideas for your practice
ConnectionsRead · 3 min

One project. One record.

When starting a project means entering the same information repeatedly, connecting intake to folders, documents and tasks can give the practice more capacity.

Custom applicationsRead · 3 min

Answer without rebuilding the project story.

If answering a client means cross-checking messages and spreadsheets, a view of milestones and outstanding items can bring together the context for a clear response.

Custom applicationsRead · 3 min

Do you need an app, or a clearer tracking process?

A spreadsheet can remain useful. A custom app becomes relevant when reconstructing project status starts to limit responses, coordination or the practice’s capacity.

DocumentsRead · 3 min

Outstanding items need a date too.

An outstanding document can hold up a delivery. Connected requests, reminders and status records make the blocker visible before the scheduled delivery date arrives.

Project coordinationRead · 3 min

From a change request to a traceable decision

Recording a request, its decision and the affected version makes the required work and delivery implications visible. Automating that workflow must preserve the approval process.

Milestones and documentationRead · 3 min

The work is delivered. What is missing to prepare invoicing?

Delivery and administrative preparation can follow different paths. Connecting statuses and evidence makes outstanding items visible and prepares the required information according to the commission’s agreed criteria.

Follow a real task before adding another platform. Identify where it stalls and which delivery, response or commission it affects.

One small first change. A clear way to move forward.
