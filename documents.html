01

The starting point

A project shares documents across several people. Versions and approvals travel through email, folders and messages. Finding what has been approved requires checking several places.

02

What we need to understand

Which review holds things up? Who needs to approve it? What happens to the deadline or next delivery when that decision is missing?

03

The improvement we design

A workflow that collects information, generates recurring documents from validated data and triggers status-based reminders. Approvals requiring judgement remain with the responsible people. We start with your documents and tools.

04

How to observe progress

Time from submission to approval, repeated reviews and pending documents. We agree what to measure and compare the change against the starting point.
