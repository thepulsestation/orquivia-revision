How this version of the site works.

No analytics or advertising

This website does not install analytics or advertising cookies. Images, fonts and icons are served from the site itself. No tracking pixels or third-party font services are loaded.

The form prepares an email

Your answers remain on the page while you prepare and review the message. The form does not send them to a server or save them in local storage. Choosing “Open my email” transfers them to your configured email application. You decide whether to send the message.

Previous preferences

An earlier version may have stored orquiviaCookieConsent and orquiviaLoaderSeenAt preferences. This version does not use them. You can remove them with the site data in your browser.
