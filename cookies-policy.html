How data is handled when you browse, contact us and suggest a call.

1. Data controller

The controller for enquiries received by Orquivia is Orquivia Global SL, Spanish tax ID B26801696, at Calle Lleida 18, 2da, Sant Joan Despí, 08970, Barcelona, España. Contact info@orquivia.com for privacy matters.

2. Data you provide and how the form works

Preparing an enquiry asks for your name, work email, business or project and a workflow description. Focus areas and suggested date and time are optional. Required fields identify the enquiry and allow a reply; you can instead email us directly.

Answers are used within the page to compose your message. This form does not send them to a server or save them in local storage. You can review, edit and choose whether to open your email or copy the message. Orquivia receives the data when you send the email.

Send only information needed to explain the workflow. Do not include passwords or sensitive or confidential third-party information. Acknowledging this policy does not subscribe you to marketing.

3. Purposes and legal bases

Received data is used to reply to your enquiry, arrange a conversation and, if requested, prepare a proposal. If you request pre-contractual steps on your own behalf, the basis is GDPR Article 6(1)(b). For contacts representing a business, the basis is the legitimate interest in responding to professional enquiries and maintaining that communication (Article 6(1)(f)).

An enquiry does not automatically add you to a marketing list. This website does not make automated decisions with legal effects on you or create advertising profiles.

4. Retention

Messages and proposals are kept while needed to handle the request and follow-up. If no engagement is agreed, they are deleted when no longer needed, except for information that must be retained for legal obligations or potential claims.

If a service is ordered, applicable legal and contractual retention periods will govern its records. Where required, information will be restricted during liability periods and deleted afterwards. Hosting provider logs follow the retention criteria in its privacy policy.

5. Providers and transfers

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub states that visitors’ IP addresses are logged for security purposes, including when not signed in. This technical logging is distinct from installing commercial analytics on this website.

GitHub processes data in the United States and other countries. Its policy describes mechanisms such as standard contractual clauses and the Data Privacy Framework where applicable. Its policy explains those safeguards and how to exercise rights with GitHub.

Orquivia’s business email uses Arsys. When an enquiry is sent, the sender’s and recipient’s email services handle transmission and storage. We do not sell enquiry data. Authorities may also receive information where legally required.

  • GitHub Pages visitor data information
  • Arsys legal and privacy information

6. Your rights

You may request access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection and, where applicable, portability by emailing info@orquivia.com. Specify the right and information needed to locate the enquiry. Additional identity information will only be requested if there are reasonable doubts.

Where processing relies on consent, you may withdraw it without affecting the lawfulness of earlier processing. Requests will be handled within statutory deadlines, normally one month. If you believe your rights have not been respected, you may complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency or the competent supervisory authority.

  • Spanish Data Protection Agency

7. Cookies and browser storage

This version’s code does not install analytics, advertising or tracking cookies or store form answers in the browser. Fonts, images and icons are served by the site. No third-party embedded maps, videos, calendars or tracking pixels are loaded.

Accordingly, this version does not show a consent panel for optional cookies it does not use. If non-essential technologies are introduced, their purposes, providers and duration will be disclosed and consent requested before activation, with options to accept, reject and change the choice.

Your browser allows cookies and stored data for this domain to be viewed and deleted, including remnants of earlier versions. Deleting them does not delete emails already sent or hosting provider logs.

8. Calls and external services

The form’s suggested date is included in the email and requires confirmation. This website does not read your calendar or access your email account.

If you follow a link to an external booking service, you will visit its website and its privacy information applies. This website does not load that service automatically. Review its data use and terms before confirming a booking.

9. Updates and contact

This policy describes the current version. It will be updated if forms, providers or technologies change. For any questions about your data, contact info@orquivia.com.
