01

The starting point

Your team uses familiar platforms, but some information is copied manually between them. A change does not always reach every place it is needed.

02

What we need to understand

Which data is repeated? Which system is the source of truth? Which permissions, checks or platform restrictions must be respected?

03

The improvement we design

A connection map and a bounded workflow that can be tested. Each integration depends on the platform’s capabilities and permissions; it is validated before extending scope.

04

How to observe progress

Duplicate entries, manual corrections and transfer time. We include review criteria so your team understands what happened and when to intervene.

Starting a commission.

If this happens
A new commission is accepted and its data is entered again in the tracking sheet, folders and templates.
What Orquivia can implement
An intake record that, once the data is validated, creates folders, prepares templates and records initial tasks in compatible tools.
Intended outcome
Start the commission with consistent information and fewer repeated steps, so more projects do not multiply administrative setup.
